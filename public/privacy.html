Privacy Policy

Morae Planner · Last updated: DRAFT

The short version
  • Your planner data belongs to you. It's used only to run the planner and sync it between your devices.
  • No ads, no tracking or analytics, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
  • You can use the planner without an account, in which case your data stays on your device.
  • You can ask for your account and data to be deleted at any time.

Who we are

Planner is a study and work planner with time boxing, a focus timer and daily reflection. It is made and run by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). You can reach us through the contact form inside the app (Settings → Contact & feedback).

What we collect

WhatWhyWhen
Email addressTo create your account, let you sign in and reset your password.Only if you create an account to sync.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising ID, or browsing activity, and we don't use analytics or tracking cookies.

Where your data is stored

  • On your device. The planner keeps a copy of your data in your browser or app storage so it works offline. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app removes this copy.
  • Supabase stores account and synced planner data. Each account can only read and change its own data. Supabase servers may be located outside your country.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, it may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.
  • Web3Forms delivers contact-form messages to our inbox by email.

Android app permissions

  • Notifications: to tell you when a focus session or break ends.
  • Alarms and reminders: so those notifications arrive on time even when the app is in the background.
  • Keep screen on: optional, so the timer stays visible while you focus.
  • Internet: to sync your data when you are signed in.

How we use your data

Only to run the planner: signing you in, syncing your data, answering your messages and fixing bugs. We don't use your data for advertising or sell it, and we only share it with the service providers listed above, who process it on our behalf to run the planner.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and synced data while your account exists. To delete your account and all synced data, send a request through the contact form using the email address on your account. We'll confirm when it's done. Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and follow up.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you can withdraw your consent by deleting your account. You can also export your planner data at any time from Settings. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can contact us, or the privacy regulator where you live (in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner).

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, and database rules make sure each account can only access its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use elsewhere.

Children

The planner is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll also let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: use Settings → Contact & feedback in the planner.
