          How to use this planner

          1. Evening, plan tomorrow. Press Tomorrow, add tasks with estimated minutes, then drag across the Plan cells to paint time blocks. The time bar turns red if the tasks don’t fit before bedtime.
          2. During the day, start the timer. Press ▶ on a task, or pick one on the Focus tab. Big screen puts the timer on a second monitor; Space pauses.
          3. The log fills itself. Every focus session lands in the Actual column. You’re nudged if a break runs long or a planned block starts with no timer running.
          4. End of day, review. When your evening review block starts, open Review: compare plan and actual, tick habits, write one line about the day, and add tomorrow’s tasks.
          5. Look back. History shows each week and month, with a heat map of how closely you followed the plan.
